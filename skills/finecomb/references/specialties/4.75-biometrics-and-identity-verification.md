# 4.75 Biometrics and identity verification

Applies to: biometric login and unlock, liveness checks, face and voice matching, document verification, know-your-customer (KYC) flows and integrations with verification vendors. Apply your own knowledge of the specific platforms, SDKs and vendors to each row.

| Checkpoint | What counts as a problem |
| --- | --- |
| **Spoofing and liveness** | Matching accepts photos, recordings, masks or synthetic media; liveness runs only on the client; fake camera feeds from virtual cameras or emulators are not detected; match thresholds are set for convenience without a measured false-accept rate |
| **Results reported by the client** | The server accepts "verified" or "matched" from the app or SDK without its own check; a biometric success on the device unlocks a server session without being bound to a key held in hardware (see [4.49](4.49-mobile-app-components-and-inter-process-communication.md) ("Local biometrics")) |
| **Vendor results and callbacks** | The vendor's result is taken from a browser redirect or an unsigned callback; the result is not tied to the user, session and attempt that started it, so a pass for one person counts for another account (see [4.24](4.24-webhooks-and-external-events.md) ("Business binding")) |
| **Fallback paths** | When the biometric or verification fails, is unavailable or is skipped, a weaker path such as a PIN, email, support agent or manual review gives the same access; enrolling a new biometric needs less proof than using one (see [4.5](4.5-authentication-sessions-and-tokens.md) ("Account recovery")) |
| Replay of captured sessions | Captured images, videos, documents or verification tokens can be sent again, for another account or after they expire; no fresh challenge is tied to each attempt (see [facets](../facets/consistency-and-completeness.md) "Uniqueness and single use") |
| Document checks | Only the picture of a document is checked, not its chip or other data from the issuer; data read from the document is trusted or parsed without checks; attempts are not limited, so forgeries can be tuned (see [46](../dimensions/46-abuse-and-fraud-resistance.md) ("Fake and mass accounts")) |
| Templates and identity data at rest | Biometric templates, raw images and document scans are stored unencrypted, in a reversible form, next to the identity, in logs or in vendor storage; unlike a password, a leaked biometric cannot be changed |
| Retention and consent | Biometric and identity data is kept longer than needed, used for other purposes, shared with vendors without consent, or not deleted when the account closes (see [30](../dimensions/30-privacy-data-governance-and-compliance.md) ("Data subject rights and consent")) |
