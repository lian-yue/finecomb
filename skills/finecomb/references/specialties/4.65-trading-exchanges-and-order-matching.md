# 4.65 Trading, exchanges and order matching

Applies to: order entry and order management, matching engines, brokerage back ends, and crypto or securities exchanges, including their market data, fees, settlement and custody. Apply your own knowledge of the specific market, its rules and its order protocols to each row.

| Checkpoint | What counts as a problem |
| --- | --- |
| **Pre-trade checks on every path** | Price bands, quantity and value limits, balance, margin and position checks are skipped or weaker on some order paths (API, batch, amend, admin, algorithmic or liquidation orders); funds are checked and reserved in separate steps, so concurrent orders spend the same balance (see [4.39](4.39-payments-accounting-and-billing.md) ("Concurrent balance updates")) |
| **Cancel, amend and fill races** | A cancel or amend and a fill are handled in an order that fills more than the remaining quantity, releases funds still in use, or leaves the account state different from the book; an amend changes price or quantity without running the checks again |
| Matching determinism and fairness | Priority is broken by amends, special order types or processing order that differs between replicas; replaying the input log does not rebuild the same book; self-trade prevention is missing or skipped for linked accounts |
| Market data and timing | Some participants get market data, fills or other users' orders earlier than others and can act on them; prices from thin books or self-matched trades feed liquidations, stops or indexes; timestamps come from clocks that can go backward (see [facets](../facets/state-timing-and-paths.md) "Choosing after seeing") |
| **Fees and rounding direction** | Rounding of price, quantity, fees, interest or funding does not always favor the venue, so many small orders or partial fills create value; tick and lot sizes are not enforced; fee rebates can be looped (see [15](../dimensions/15-algorithm-and-data-structure-correctness.md) ("Rounding direction")) |
| Settlement and reconciliation | Trades, balances and positions in the matching engine, the ledger and custody are not reconciled, or a difference does not stop withdrawals; a failed settlement leaves only one side updated (see [4.39](4.39-payments-accounting-and-billing.md) ("Reconciliation")) |
| Circuit breakers and kill switches | A market, an account or an algorithm cannot be halted quickly; a halt misses some order paths; a market reopens without checking the orders left on the book (see [29](../dimensions/29-fail-safe-behavior-and-dangerous-operations.md) ("Emergency controls")) |
| **Custody of hot and cold funds** | Hot wallets or shared accounts hold more than daily needs; withdrawals are not limited, delayed or reviewed by amount and destination; deposits are credited before they are final (see [4.43](4.43-wallets-signing-and-off-chain-components.md)) |
