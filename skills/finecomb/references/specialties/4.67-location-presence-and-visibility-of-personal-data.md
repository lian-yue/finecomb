# 4.67 Location, presence and visibility of personal data

Applies to: location sharing and "nearby" features, online status, typing indicators and read receipts, friend and follower graphs, contact discovery, and audience and visibility settings in social and communication products. Apply your own knowledge of the specific platform and its known privacy leaks to each row.

| Checkpoint | What counts as a problem |
| --- | --- |
| **Precision of location and distance** | Exact coordinates or distances reach the client and are hidden only by the interface; rounded or fuzzed values can be narrowed down by repeated queries from locations the caller sets; location stays in photos, files or other metadata |
| Presence and activity signals | Online status, last seen, typing, read receipts and activity times reveal routines or who talks to whom, or still show after the person turned them off in one place |
| **Visibility settings on every path** | Audience and privacy settings are applied in the main view but not in search, APIs, exports, notifications, previews, embeds, caches or third-party apps (see [28](../dimensions/28-authorization-and-access-control.md) ("Consistency across entry points")) |
| **Blocked and restricted users** | A blocked or restricted person can still see or reach the user through another feature, a second account, a shared group or space, or a logged-out view (see [46](../dimensions/46-abuse-and-fraud-resistance.md) ("Harassment and unwanted contact")) |
| Audience changes after posting | Narrowing a post's or profile's audience, deleting it or leaving a group does not reach copies already shared, cached, indexed, sent in notifications or synced |
| **Inference from counts and discovery** | Counts, suggestions, mutual connections, contact discovery by phone number or email, and different error messages reveal hidden accounts, contacts or relationships; contact discovery can be queried in bulk (see [facets](../facets.md) "Observable differences") |
| Defaults and expiry | New users and new features start with wide visibility; live location or status sharing has no end time and no reminder that it is still on; data from these features is kept longer than needed (see [30](../dimensions/30-privacy-data-governance-and-compliance.md) ("Minimization")) |
