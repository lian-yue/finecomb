# 16 Business logic and concurrency

| Pattern | Historical cases | What to look for | Where it lands |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Races on limits** | Concurrent repeated use of coupons, withdrawals and points; concurrent requests in a single packet can trigger it reliably (the "single-packet attack" technique published in 2023) | Whether checking the quota and deducting it happen in the same atomic operation | [9](../dimensions/9-business-logic-and-flow-integrity.md) ("Timing and races"), [23](../dimensions/23-transactions-atomicity-and-consistency.md) |
| Negative, zero and overflowing quantities | Placing orders with negative quantities; amounts that become smaller after overflow | Whether the server validates the ranges of quantities and amounts | [9](../dimensions/9-business-logic-and-flow-integrity.md) ("Quantities and quotas"), [14](../dimensions/14-boundaries-numbers-and-text.md) |
| Skipped steps | Jumping straight past payment and verification steps to the last step | Whether the server enforces the step order | [9](../dimensions/9-business-logic-and-flow-integrity.md) ("Step order") |
| Intermediate states of multi-step flows exploited | GitLab (CVE-2022-4037, a race in the email verification flow allowed forging a verified email, and then taking over third-party accounts that sign in with GitLab) | Whether the state between two steps can be exploited with concurrent requests; whether the "verified" flag and the verified value are updated together atomically | [12](../dimensions/12-state-machines-and-transitions.md) ("Transition atomicity"), [9](../dimensions/9-business-logic-and-flow-integrity.md) ("Timing and races") |
| **Connections get out of step after cancellation, and responses go to someone else** | redis-py (CVE-2023-28858, CVE-2023-28859, after an async command was canceled at the wrong moment, the connection was still reused; in March 2023 this made ChatGPT show some users' conversation titles and payment information to other users) | On cancel and timeout paths, whether the connection is closed or returned to the pool; whether reuse first confirms there is no leftover response | [4.1](../specialties/4.1-networking-and-connections.md) ("Connection state after cancellation"), [4.6](../specialties/4.6-caching-and-storage.md) ("Cache keys and isolation") |
