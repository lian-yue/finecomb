# Held-out round 1 and retest

This record lists every sample of held-out round 1, plus the retest done in the same run, so they can be looked up later by ID, component or root-cause facet. The summary numbers are in the main README under [How coverage is validated](https://github.com/lian-yue/finecomb#how-coverage-is-validated).

## What this round is

- Held-out samples: group B, 31 vulnerabilities that were not used to write the skill. Three sample groups: 15 from the Linux kernel; 8 old nginx advisories; 8 from Apache httpd, HAProxy, Envoy and OpenSSL. They count toward the held-out statistics in the main README.
- Retest samples: group A, 14 vulnerabilities used in the derivation round (the round whose samples were used to find gaps while the skill was written). In the derivation round they were partial or missed, and the skill was extended based on them; this retest checks whether the added text now reaches them. Group A has its own section and does not count toward the held-out statistics.
- Test date: 2026-09-24. The skill under test was the Chinese skill `skills/finecomb-zh/` as it stood then (files modified 22:47–22:53, unchanged after judging started). Row names below use the corresponding names in the English skill `skills/finecomb/`.
- Root causes: one sentence at the patch or official advisory level. For group B they come from three read-only research agents that read the patches or official advisories, all rated high confidence; group A reuses the derivation-round notes.

## Judging rules

- Only the "Question to ask" column of the root-cause facets (facets.md) and the "Checkpoint" and "What counts as a problem" columns of the tables in dimensions.md, specialties.md and lang-*.md are used.
- The "Real cases" column of `facets.md` is not used, and neither are rows in `history/` that name the same CVE.
- Hit: following some row's question leads directly to the root cause.
- Weak hit: existing rows do reach the root cause, but only by combining two or three rows, or through one particular word. Still counted as a hit, written "Hit (weak)" in the tables.
- Partial: the rows only point in the general direction; the reviewer still has to take another step on their own.
- Missed: no row leads to the root cause.

## Other skill problems found in this round (not per sample)

The top of facets.md says the "Detailed criteria" column of each facet points to specific check rows, but at the time the links of six facets did not land on rows that actually held the criteria: "Data layout assumptions", "Fallbacks and rarely taken paths", "Lifetime and ownership", "Uniqueness and single use", "Assumptions broken by callbacks and reentrancy" and "Which attributes survive a move". A reviewer following the links could not find the criteria.

Where it was fixed: new rows dimensions 14 "Data layout", dimensions 13 "Fallback branches", and dimensions 19 "Objects invalidated within a batch" and "Allocation ownership"; the links of these facets now point to the rows that actually hold the criteria.
